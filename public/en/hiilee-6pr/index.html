Core solution

The HIILEE-6PR Culture of Wholeness

Six capacities for wholesome happiness (HIILEE) × six lasting outcomes (6P), driven by the 6R transformation cycle.

HIILEE-6PR is a simple, effective tool for building an ideal culture for individuals, families and organizations, summarized in one formula:

HIILEE-6P capacity for wholesome happiness × 6R transformation cycle ⇔ HIILEE-6PR Culture of Wholeness

The six capacity-outcome pairs (HIILEE-6P)

Health - Predictability

Health is the basic foundation for success in every field. When we maintain both physical and mental health, we can face unexpected challenges from the outside world - which brings peace of mind through Predictability: better focus, better planning, more accurate decisions.

Ideal - Purpose

An Ideal is the human longing for something complete and exemplary - a positive form of imagination more realistic than a dream, blending reality and romance. When we live by an ideal, what we do naturally becomes meaningful and carries a noble Purpose - and doing meaningful work reinforces the ideal in turn.

Integrity - Prestige

Integrity is not only honesty and respect for reality, but also fairness and empathy in judgment, keeping one's word, daring to act and to take responsibility. With integrity in how we think and treat others, we gradually earn trust - that is Prestige.

Learning - Progress

Learning is the ongoing practice of reflecting on real experience and listening openly to diverse views, drawing lessons about both events and ourselves. Past a certain level of success, learning to unlearn - leaving the raft behind after crossing the river - matters too. Learning this way, individuals and organizations make steady Progress and grow sustainably.

Empathy - Prosperity

Following Otto Scharmer's Theory U, generative listening is the deepest level of listening - beyond empathizing, it reaches toward understanding identity, envisioning the future and driving change. The capacity for Empathy is therefore the key to Prosperity - wealth in finances and in quality relationships - for individuals, families and businesses alike.

Enjoyment - Persistence

Enjoyment means savoring not only the process of work and its achievements, but also setbacks and challenges - cheerfully accepting the imperfections in ourselves and others, the way we admire the beauty of a crescent moon. Enjoying each small step forward fuels Persistence on the road to big goals.

The 6R transformation cycle

To become ever more wholesomely happy, self-transformation needs to be synchronized (change at the level of belief, not just words), manageable (minimizing the pain of facing where we fall short) and regular (a steady rhythm, like brushing your teeth every day).

  1. R1 - Reflection: look back at your desires, emotions, what you've achieved and what you want to make more whole.
  2. R2 - Raise the bar: set the specific goal you most want to reach in the next 3 months.
  3. R3 - Role model: find someone who has done something similar.
  4. R4 - Resource: assess your time, money and current capacity for wholesome happiness.
  5. R5 - Relation: to go far and fast, travel with Self Hiil and the Hiilee community.
  6. R6 - Routine: keep a steady learning rhythm with the community - success doesn't come from improvisation!
