HIILEE 10x - The Constructive Listening flight

Unlocks the Empathy - Prosperity pair. Following Otto Scharmer's Theory U, generative listening is the deepest level of listening - the Empathy-Prosperity key for individuals, families and businesses.

You only need 3.5 hours aboard the HIILEE 10x “flight” with the 3T coaching model. The flight guides you through three pivotal questions:

  • What is the true nature of listening?
  • Which critical misconception makes you mis-hear?
  • Where is your activation point for generative listening?

HIILEE 20x - The Self-Wisdom flight

Unlocks two pairs: Integrity - Prestige and Learning - Progress. The key is recognizing and choosing self-arising wisdom - not accumulated knowledge, but the capacity to perceive according to truth.

In 3.5 hours, the HIILEE 20x flight guides you through three pivotal questions:

  • What is the true nature of self-arising wisdom?
  • Which critical misconception keeps you from using it?
  • Where is your activation point for self-arising wisdom?

HIILEE 30x - The “Who am I?” flight

Unlocks three pairs: Ideal - Purpose, Enjoyment - Persistence and Health - Predictability. The key is using self-arising wisdom to explore the big question - “Who am I?” - in just 3.5 hours of flight.
