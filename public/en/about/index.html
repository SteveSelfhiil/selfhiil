Our story

About Self Hiil

Self. Hiil - Heel & Heal. Resolving the “Achilles heel” for wholesome happiness.

Self Hiil was founded in 2020. The name combines “Self” with “Hiil” - a phonetic blend of Heel and Heal. We believe that no matter how talented and virtuous a person may be, everyone has an “Achilles heel” - a critical weakness that needs to be resolved and healed.

With the HIILEE-6PR Culture of Wholeness model, Self Hiil continuously researches and creates tools built to the 3T standard (Speed - Essence - Wholeness) to help everyone amplify their strengths and transform their weaknesses for an ever more wholesome happiness.

Why we exist

“At Self Hiil, we believe that deep inside every person is a need for wholesome happiness. Everyone wants a healthier body and mind, a closer and more abundant family, a career that breaks through with meaning. The only difference is who sees it, admits it, and acts on it first.”

Founder Nguyễn Thùy Liên

Self Hiil doesn't just understand this legitimate need - we have developed the HIILEE-6PR “formula” so you can enjoy wholesome happiness sooner, across self, family and career.

Our core solution

The HIILEE-6PR Culture of Wholeness is a simple, effective tool for building an ideal culture for individuals, families and organizations. It combines foundational ideas and a shared thinking framework for wholesome happiness (HIILEE-6P) with toolkits and principles to apply that wholeness to everyday life (6R).

The whole solution can be summarized in one formula:

HIILEE-6P capacity for wholesome happiness × 6R transformation cycle ⇔ HIILEE-6PR Culture of Wholeness

HIILEE-6PR is easy to use, fast, and transforms both mindset and behavior. It is a breakthrough for building family culture - and a hopeful path for Vietnamese businesses to replicate the founder's “core” in the next generation of leaders.
