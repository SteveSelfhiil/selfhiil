The Self Hiil story

Why “Self Hiil”?

Self Hiil was founded in 2020. The name combines “Self” with “Hiil” - a phonetic blend of Heel and Heal. We believe that no matter how talented and virtuous a person is, everyone has an “Achilles heel” - a critical weakness that needs to be resolved and healed. With the HIILEE-6PR Culture of Wholeness model, Self Hiil continuously researches and creates tools built to the 3T standard (Speed - Essence - Wholeness) to help everyone amplify their strengths and transform their weaknesses for an ever more wholesome happiness.

Core solution

The HIILEE-6PR Culture of Wholeness

HIILEE-6PR is a simple, effective tool for building an ideal culture for individuals, families and organizations: six capacities for wholesome happiness (HIILEE) paired with six lasting outcomes (6P), driven by the 6R transformation cycle.

HIILEE-6P capacity for wholesome happiness × 6R transformation cycle ⇔ HIILEE-6PR Culture of Wholeness
H

Health - Predictability

Physical and mental wellbeing - the foundation of peace of mind and a future you can count on.

I

Ideal - Purpose

Discovering and pursuing what you both love and find useful - so work and life carry real meaning.

I

Integrity - Prestige

Respecting reality, acting with honesty and fairness - the foundation of trust and reputation.

L

Learning - Progress

Reflecting, drawing lessons and correcting course from real experience - so you and your organization keep growing.

E

Empathy - Prosperity

Deeply understanding your own and others' emotions and needs - the key to prosperity in finances and relationships.

E

Enjoyment - Persistence

Savoring life, work and even challenges - the energy source of persistence toward your goals.

The 6R cycle: Reflection → Raise the bar → Role model → Resource → Relation → Routine.
